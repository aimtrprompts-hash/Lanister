## 1.0

- Lannister is a new launcher built from Plume Launcher. All Plume features stay.
- The screensaver shows slow full-screen photos, a large clock and your city. It can also show what is playing.
- **Live sky** is a new wallpaper and screensaver. The sky changes from dawn to day, dusk and night with the real sunrise and sunset. The sun, moon and stars cross it.
- The Live sky shows your weather: clouds, rain, snow, fog and storms. Select **Lannister settings → Light wallpaper** or **Dark wallpaper** to choose it.
- Select **Lannister settings → Sky speed → Fast** to see a whole day in 2 minutes.
- Select **Lannister settings → Screensaver** to turn it on. Select **Now playing** to show the song and artist.
- Lannister can replace the Amazon home screen on a Fire TV. Select **Lannister settings → Set up Home button**.
- Lannister runs on Fire OS 6 (Android 7.1). Continue Watching and HDMI tiles need Android 8 and stay empty there.
- Lannister does not update itself. Install a new APK to update.
- The wallpaper switches between a Nairobi day and night skyline using Nairobi time (Africa/Nairobi), with a fade. Day starts at 06:30 and night at 18:30 by default. Change this in **Lannister settings → Nairobi and widgets**.
- The built-in Nairobi skyline is drawn, not a photograph. To use real photos, add `nairobi_day.jpg` and `nairobi_night.jpg`. See the README.
- A greeting shows "Good morning/afternoon/evening/night, Sir Mitch", following Nairobi time. The clock is always 24-hour, in Nairobi time.
- A small music widget in the top bar shows the song, artist and artwork from the playing app, with previous, play/pause and next buttons and a small equalizer. It needs the same notification access as the screensaver's Now playing.
